Hotel Lakeside

A self-initiated redesign of Hotel Lakeside's landing page, focused on typography, content refinement and visual presentation.

Role
UI Designer
Platform
Web
Tools
Figma · Notion
View Figma prototype

01. Overview

A fresh look for an existing landing page

I revisited Hotel Lakeside's landing page as a personal design exercise, exploring a more contemporary visual direction for the hotel in Parentis-en-Born, France.

The work focused on typography, content refinement and visual presentation. I retained much of the original section order and alternating image-and-text layout.

02. What I changed

A focused visual refresh

01

Typography

Primarily Rubik and Poppins replace the original's decorative typography, with size and weight distinguishing headings, body text and actions.

02

Content refinement

I shortened selected paragraphs and refined headings and button labels, using information from the original page.

03

Visual styling

Updated imagery, spacing, rounded image corners and button styling establish the visual direction. Warm brown accents connect headings and actions throughout the page.

04

Section updates

Three image cards introduce the hotel, restaurant and lounge. The rooftop and contact sections also received updated styling.

03. Design considerations

Imagery and hero treatment

The replacement imagery explores a more luxurious visual direction. These illustrative photographs do not depict Hotel Lakeside or its facilities.

The original page uses a video hero. I used a static image in this concept because video was unavailable in my Figma free-account setup.

04. Reflection

What I practised

This exercise helped me practise typography, content hierarchy and visual consistency within an existing landing page structure.
